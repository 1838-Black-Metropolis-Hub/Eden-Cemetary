Eden Cemetery · Self-Guided Walk Full Walk · Eight stops
Take the walk on your phone Scan for the full narrative, photos & map edencemetery.org
Entrance: Springfield Road
STOP 1Walk stop (a section, not a single grave) Grave discussed on the walk
Stop 1
Celestine Named for Celestine Moseley Cromwell, Eden’s first burial (August 14, 1902) after white residents tried to block her funeral procession.
Stop 2
Richard Allen Namesake not buried here. Founder of the AME Church; with Absalom Jones began the Free African Society (1787), which cared for the sick and the dead. Arizona Cleaver Stemons, a founder of Zeta Phi Beta Sorority, Inc., is buried here.
Stop 3
Lehmann Martin J. Lehmann (1859–1924), cigar maker and treasurer — one of the five founders who chartered, defended, and kept the records of Eden. Note architect Julian Abele and Pauline Oberdorfer Minor, a founder of Delta Sigma Theta Sorority, Inc.
Stop 4
Lebanon An 1849 Philadelphia cemetery, robbed of bodies in 1882 and condemned in 1899; its dead came to Eden in 1903. Forten, Bias, Catto, LeCount, Still, Caroline Still Anderson, the Mossells, Laura Wheeler Waring.
Stop 5
John Brown Named for the abolitionist, not buried here. Frances Ellen Watkins Harper, poet and Still’s colleague, buried here in 1911.
Stop 6
Bowers Named for John C. Bowers (d. 1873), transferred from Lebanon. His sister Henrietta Bowers Duterte was a pioneering undertaker; the family graves lie in Lebanon.
Stop 7
Olive An 1849 West Philadelphia cemetery linked to Stephen Smith, transferred to Eden in 1923. William Whipper, Jessie Redmon Fauset, Arthur Huff Fauset.
Stop 8
Celestine · return Return: Rebecca J. Cole, Matthew Anderson, Marian Anderson, Charles L. Blockson — later burials extending Eden across a century.
Uneven ground — proceed into the lots with caution. Follow the stops in order. This walk discusses racism, grave disturbance, and the movement of human remains. Eden is an active cemetery — please respect graves, services, and other visitors. Eden Cemetery · 1434 Springfield Rd, Collingdale PA 19023 · 610‑583‑8737
